## Scope

This procedure covers replacement of fictional Nexora Pay physical consumer cards because they are lost, stolen, damaged, retained by an ATM, or near expiry. It does not define virtual-card replacement, business cards, or delivery outside the European Economic Area.

## Immediate safety step

For a lost or stolen card, instruct the customer to freeze the card in the app immediately. If the customer cannot access the app, use the 24/7 emergency support flow. A stolen card with an unrecognized transaction is a fraud case and must follow the Fraud Escalation Policy.

The available demo tools cannot freeze or replace a card. They may retrieve an approved customer summary, retrieve service status, or create a support ticket. Never imply that creating a ticket has frozen the card.

## Replacement reasons

| Reason | Fee | Handling |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Stolen | EUR 0 | Freeze first; fraud screening required |
| Lost | EUR 0 for the first replacement in 12 months | Freeze first; verify delivery address |
| Damaged | EUR 6 | Existing card may remain active until activation of the new card |
| Retained by ATM | EUR 0 | Record ATM location and time; do not advise physical retrieval |
| Expiring | EUR 0 | Automatic replacement begins 30 calendar days before expiry |

## Standard delivery targets

Targets start after identity and address checks are complete:

- Germany: **4-6 business days**.
- Other supported European Economic Area destinations: **7-12 business days**.
- Outside the European Economic Area: not defined by this procedure; escalate rather than inventing a target.

The Germany target conflicts with the 5-7-business-day quick reference in the Customer Support Policy. A definitive answer about that route requires human review with both citations.

## Express delivery

Express replacement is available to a previously verified address in Germany for **EUR 14.90** and targets **1-2 business days**. Express delivery is unavailable during a new-address cooldown, for unverified identity, or for destinations outside Germany.

## Address controls

- Confirm only a masked form of the previously verified address.
- An address changed during the preceding 24 hours cannot be used for replacement delivery.
- Do not accept a new address solely through free-form chat.
- If the old address is unsafe and the new address is in cooldown, send the case for human review.

## Required case record

Record the replacement reason, card status, verification outcome, masked delivery destination, delivery tier, fee disclosure, and whether any transactions are unrecognized. Do not record a full card number or CVV.

## Customer communication

Delivery targets are estimates, not guarantees. State the applicable range, the point at which timing begins, and any unresolved source conflict. A delayed delivery normally uses P3 unless the facts meet a higher-risk rule.
